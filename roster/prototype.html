8 findings · click a pin or a card

The live Roster page as an admin sees it before uploading. Each pin is a finding from the written critique, paired with the method I would use to confirm it and a link to how the target-state flow handles it. Pink means here what it means everywhere in this prototype: something I changed.

1Inverted

The pitch is inverted

The banner sells a membership tier and a discount code. It never says what a roster does: tell the store which swimmers exist, what gear their group requires, what sizes to collect, and who to remind before a deadline. Membership is what we earn once that works.

How I'd validate it Ask growth whether this copy is tested or shipped, then A/B job-first against perk-first on the same banner.

2Confusing

"Create a New Roster" has no stated purpose

A team with no roster yet is offered a second one. Nothing explains when you would want more than one, and nothing offers the obvious shortcut for a returning team: last season's roster.

How I'd validate it Session replay on the Roster page for new stores — how many click it, and what they do next.

3Missing

Add Member accepts a member who can never be reached

The modal saves a member with no email address, silently creating a roster row that cannot receive the benefit the banner just promised. Nothing marks the field as required.

How I'd validate it Data pull on the share of existing roster rows with no family email; ticket review for "my families never got the code".

4Confusing

Four date fields, no stated purpose or requirement

They sit above the roster with no indication of whether they block the upload, what they drive downstream, or what to do when the schedule is not set yet in August.

How I'd validate it Interviews with three coaches: is the season calendar even known at the moment they set up the store?

5Confusing

Three surfaces, three vocabularies, no required marks

The grid says Athlete First Name and Family Email. The template says Account First Name and Email. Add Member asks for Swimmer Name. The help article uses a fourth set. Nothing here marks the three fields that actually gate an upload.

How I'd validate it Support ticket review: which field names admins use when they describe what went wrong.

6Missing

No mapping, no preview, no partial success

If headers do not match, the only path is reshaping the file by hand in Excel. Nothing shows what we understood before committing, and nothing communicates that 300 of 312 rows were fine.

How I'd validate it Validation failures by class, plus an audit of 30 real files from the support queue.

7Missing

Only two of six source systems are addressed

Teams on Hy-Tek, SwimCloud, SportsEngine and parent-maintained Sheets are the population most likely to fail validation, and the page speaks only to the two exports that already work.

How I'd validate it Audit real attachments in support tickets, segmented by source system.

8Confusing

The guidance sits after the moment it is needed

The three how-to guides are below the upload button and below the fold. An admin who needed to prepare her file differently finds that out after she has already failed.

How I'd validate it Session replay: scroll depth on the Roster page and click-through on the guide links.
